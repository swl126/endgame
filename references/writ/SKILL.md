# Writ: Writing That Doesn't Read as AI-Generated

Integrated into ENDGAME as an optional writing-quality specialist, derived from Avinashricky211/writ under the MIT License.

## Core principle

The biggest writing tell is often the impulse to explain the significance of something instead of simply stating it. Avoid over-narrating, redundant summaries, manufactured contrast, and templated prose.

## Self-edit checklist

1. **Kill tacked-on significance.** Remove unnecessary endings that explain why the preceding fact matters. State the fact and trust the reader.
2. **Retire clustered stock vocabulary.** Watch for repeated terms such as delve, tapestry, robust, seamless, game-changer, cutting-edge, moreover, furthermore, underscore, leverage, foster, holistic, and similar formulaic language.
3. **Break false contrast habits.** Avoid repeated “it’s not X, it’s Y” constructions unless the contrast is genuine.
4. **Cut redundant recaps.** Do not restate points without adding information. Delete paragraphs that add no new substance.
5. **Ration em dashes.** Prefer commas, colons, parentheses, or periods unless an em dash serves a real interruption.
6. **Do not manufacture debate.** Do not add “some argue,” “opinions vary,” or similar framing around settled facts merely to sound balanced.
7. **Vary transitions.** Avoid rotating mechanically through moreover, furthermore, additionally, in conclusion, that said, and similar connectors.
8. **Avoid promotional lean in neutral contexts.** Remove unearned superlatives and press-release language when the task calls for analysis or neutrality.
9. **Use formatting with restraint.** Do not default to excessive headings, bold text, or bullets when ordinary prose reads better.
10. **Remove assistant residue from the artifact.** Phrases such as “I hope this helps,” “feel free to,” and “certainly” should not appear inside finished deliverables unless context genuinely calls for them.
11. **Vary sentence rhythm.** Mix sentence lengths and structures naturally rather than producing uniformly balanced medium-length sentences.

## Application

Apply the checklist silently as a final editorial pass. Do not narrate the checklist to the user unless asked. Do not over-correct: these are tendencies, not forbidden constructions. Match the edit to the genre; technical, legal, academic, personal, and promotional writing have different norms.
